The green triangle that protects you

Since October 2025, every SEBI-registered intermediary collecting your money must use a special UPI handle issued by NPCI — it looks like name.brk@validbank and shows a white thumbs-up inside a green triangle on your payment screen.

A scammer can type any name or registration number into a chat. They cannot make NPCI issue them a real @valid handle. That is why this tool checks the payment channel first — and treats everything else as context.

Old-style handles were required to stop collecting investments by Dec 2025 (existing MF SIPs excepted). A pitch still asking for one is a serious red flag.
